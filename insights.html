Data Story · Visualization Demo

The global economy, through its crises.

Two once-in-a-generation shocks — the 2008 financial crisis and the 2020 pandemic — reshaped the world economy. This interactive piece traces how growth, prices, and debt moved through them. Built from scratch in SVG as a taste of the data-visualization work ARYX does.

−3.1%
World GDP growth in 2020 — the deepest peacetime contraction since the 1930s.
8.7%
Global inflation peak in 2022, the highest in roughly four decades.
~99%
Global public debt as a share of GDP at the 2020 pandemic peak.

World GDP growth

Annual change in real global GDP. The two dips — 2009 and 2020 — mark the financial crisis and the pandemic.
