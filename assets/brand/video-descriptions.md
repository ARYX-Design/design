### aryx-facts-tokens-9x16
**On-screen hook:** AI chatbots don't read words.
**Caption:** AI chatbots don't read words, they read tokens: small pieces of text. A token can be a word, part of a word or punctuation, and the model writes one token at a time, predicting what comes next. That's why AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Check important facts.
**Pinned comment:** Did you know this? Comment 🤯 if it's new to you.
**Note:** the token examples on screen are labelled "simplified"; real tokenizers split text differently per model.

**Sources:** Deep Blue v Kasparov (1997); AlphaGo v Lee Sedol (March 2016, 4–1); John Tromp / Wikipedia "Go and mathematics": ≈2.08×10^170 legal 19×19 positions vs ≈10^80 atoms (the comparison counts positions, not games).
