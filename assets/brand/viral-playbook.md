# ARYX — posting playbook for short videos

No one can guarantee a video goes viral. What follows is general guidance on what short-form platforms tend to reward, not a promise of results. Treat the first 10–20 posts as experiments.

## What the five viral-style videos already do
- **Hook on frame 1:** the question is on screen and spoken immediately, with no logo intro.
- **Short:** 15–19 seconds, hard cut every 1–3 seconds.
- **Big word-by-word captions** in the middle of the screen, inside the area TikTok/Reels/Shorts do not cover (the bottom ~20% and the right edge are left clear).
- **Sound effects** on the cuts, so the video is not silent between words.
- **Loop:** each video ends by repeating the hook for ~0.8 s, so replays feel seamless. Replays and watch time are signals the platforms weigh heavily.
- **Share prompt:** each one ends with "send this to someone who…" or "save this for later", which targets shares and saves.

## When posting
1. **Cover:** use the matching file from `assets/video/covers/` (the hook with its visual).
2. **Add a trending sound at low volume inside the app** (TikTok/Reels sound library). This can help discovery and is not something that can be built into the file.
3. **Pin the first comment** from `video-descriptions.md` and reply to every comment in the first hour, ideally with a short video reply.
4. **Hashtags:** 3–5 per post (see `hashtags.md`). Check each tag's volume in the app first.
5. **Post one video a day** across TikTok, Reels and Shorts rather than all five at once. The same file works on all three.
6. **Try hooks, not just topics.** Post the same message with a different first line and compare.

## What to watch in the analytics
- **Average watch time / % watched to the end** matters more than views. If viewers leave in the first 2 seconds, rewrite the hook.
- **Shares and saves** show whether the "send this to…" prompt works.
- **Profile visits and link clicks** show whether people want to hire ARYX. Views alone do not bring clients.

## Make sure there is somewhere to land
Put `aryx.design@gmail.com` (or the website once it has a domain) in each profile's bio link. Without that, a viral video brings views but no enquiries.

## Honesty and claims
The videos only claim what ARYX offers: simple automations from €19.99, fixed prices, AI agents and websites. The "while you sleep" video is labelled as an example. Do not add results, client counts or time-saved figures unless they are real and you can show them.
